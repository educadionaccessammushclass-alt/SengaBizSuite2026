Introduce the complete bilingual SENGA Digital Operations Platform in Kiswahili and English. Includes: - Bilingual language selection and navigation. - Customer registration and management. - Service catalogue and booking management. - Service request registration and tracking. - Contract management. - Finance and payment records. - LEGAL360 legal matter register. - Geographical service coverage across eight countries. - Tanzania regional coverage. - 16 operational divisions (AT01–AT16). - IndexedDB media library for images, audio and video. - Operational reports and CSV export. - JSON database backup and restoration. - SENGA homepage with existing portrait asset (senga-homepage.jpg). STORAGE AND DEPLOYMENT: Operational records are stored in browser local storage. Media files are stored in browser IndexedDB. Cloud storage, multi-user access and automatic synchronisation between devices are not yet implemented. This release establishes the bilingual browser-based operational platform for SENGA.
